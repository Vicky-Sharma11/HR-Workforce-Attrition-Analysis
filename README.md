# HR-Workforce-Attrition-Analysis

## Dataset

IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset.
The dataset contains employee information about demographics, job roles, income, satisfaction, and attrition.

## Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze employee data and attrition using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset.
The project looks at attrition across departments, tenure groups, overtime, income, and employee satisfaction.

## Business Objectives

• Analyze employee attrition and workforce distribution.
• Compare attrition across departments.
• Examine attrition across tenure groups.
• Explore the relationship between overtime, job satisfaction, work environment, and work life balance.
• Present key findings through an interactive dashboard.

## Key Performance Indicators

• Total Employees
• Attrition Count
• Attrition Rate
• Retention Rate
• Average Income
• Average Age
• Average Tenure
• Average Tenure at Exit
• Percentage of Employees

## Key Findings

• Employees reporting poor work life balance had a higher attrition rate.
• Attrition rates differed between employees who worked overtime and those who did not.
• Attrition patterns varied across employee satisfaction levels.
• Employees in the new tenure group tend to have a higher attrition rate.
